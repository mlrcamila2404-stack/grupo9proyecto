About Us

Practice, improve and advance at your own place

Practify is a practice platform designed to help you strengthen your Listening, Reading, and Conversation skills through short exams, clear feedback, and progress tracking.

Our Mission

We believe that learning a language should not depend on long exams or feeling lost about what you need to improve. That's why we created Practify: a simple space where you can practice in parts, receive honest feedback, and see your progress reflected clearly, without pressure and at your own pace.

What You'll Find in Practify

Listening

Listening comprehension exercises with short questions to train your ear gradually.

Reading

Texts and questions focused on vocabulary, main ideas, and inferences.

Conversation

Practice oriented towards common questions and answers in real contexts.

Progress

Visualize your overall progress and performance by skill after each practice session.

Feedback

Upon completing each exam, you receive clear comments about your strengths and areas to review.

Profile

Save your history and statistics to see how much you've improved over time.

Why Practify?

  • Short practices you can complete in minutes, when you have time.
  • Honest feedback, not just a number.
  • Progress tracking by skill, not just overall.
  • No distractions: a simple interface designed to help you focus on practicing.
